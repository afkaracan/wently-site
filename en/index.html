FAQ

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone, in Wently's own storage. There is no account and no server.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup from Settings > Backup and import it on the new phone. Importing never deletes anything.

How do you count countries?

The 193 UN member states, the observer states Vatican City and Palestine, Northern Cyprus and Kosovo; England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland count separately: 200 countries in all.
