Wently Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026
Developer: Ahmet Furkan Karacan
Contact: afkaracan@gmail.com

Wently is an iPhone app for recording the cities and countries you have visited. This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it keeps it, and when it connects to the internet.

1. In short

  • No account. You do not sign up or sign in to use Wently.
  • No server of ours. Your visits, notes, photos and other records are stored only on your device. We cannot access them.
  • No ads, tracking or analytics. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Nothing sold or shared. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to sell or share.
  • The app connects to public services only to show some content (city guide, public holidays, map imagery). These requests contain nothing that identifies you (details in §5).

2. Information stored on your device

The following is kept in Wently's own storage on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your visits: city or country, dates, length of stay, visit type (trip, city you lived in), means of transport, notes and ratings.
  • Your trips, plans, wishlist, collection progress, flight log, visited airports and ports, and day-counter rules.
  • Travel companion names: names you type for a visit or trip. These are free text you enter; Wently does not access your Contacts.
  • Custom places you add (name and location).
  • Photos you add to visits: the app resizes them to at most 2048 pixels, creates a small thumbnail and removes location and other EXIF data.
  • Your settings (theme, text size, notification preferences, etc.).
  • Summary information prepared for the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (counts, a map image), kept in a shared folder on your device that only Wently and its widgets can access.
  • Caches: city guides and event lists you open are kept for 30 days, and city time zones permanently, so the same content is not downloaded again.
  • The city, country, border, airport and port database that ships with the app is read-only and contains no information about you.

Device backups: if iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on for your iPhone, Wently's data may be included in them like that of any other app. Those backups are controlled by Apple and you; we have no access to them.

3. Permissions and on-device processing

Wently asks for each permission only when you use the related feature. If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working. You can withdraw permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

| Permission | Purpose | What happens | |---|---|---| | Photos | Visit suggestions from your photos, and adding photos to a visit | The app reads where and when your photos were taken on your device, matches this against the built-in city list and shows suggestions. Photos and their locations are not sent anywhere. Only photos you choose to add to a visit are stored in the app, as resized copies with location data removed. | | Location (while using) | Suggesting the city you are in | Your location is matched on the device against the built-in city list. | | Location (always, optional) | When "Detect New Cities" is on, noticing that you arrived in a new city and suggesting the visit | Uses Apple's low-power visit and significant-location-change services at about 1 km accuracy. Your location is processed on the device only to find which city you are in; arrivals and departures are stored on the device as suggestions. Your location history is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off in Settings > Automatic import. | | Calendar | Suggesting trips from past flights, hotel stays and events with a location | Only when you tap "Find Trips in Calendar" are your events read on the device. The scan results are not written to storage; only suggestions you accept are saved as visits. Wently never writes to your calendar. | | Camera | Scanning a friend's comparison QR code | Camera frames are processed on the device only to read the QR code and are not saved. | | Notifications | Arrival in a new city, "On this day" memories, plan reminders, day-rule warnings | All notifications are local, created on the device. No push-notification server is used. |

4. iCloud

Wently does not currently sync with iCloud; your data stays on your device. If iCloud sync is added in the future, data would be stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud service, not accessible to us, and this policy will be updated.

5. Services the app connects to

Wently connects to the internet in the cases below. These requests never include your name, email address, device identifiers, advertising identifier or your location. As with any internet request, the server can see your IP address and standard connection information (such as a "User-Agent" naming the app and its version). That information is governed by the respective service's own privacy policy.

| Service | When | What is sent | |---|---|---| | Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons (Wikimedia Foundation) | When you open a city guide, "Before you go" information or events for a city | The city's article title or Wikidata ID and a language code. Images in the guide are loaded from Wikimedia servers. | | Nager.Date (nagerholidays.com) | When showing public holidays for a plan or city | A country code and a year (e.g. "TR", 2027). | | Apple Maps (MapKit) | When showing the satellite map; once per city to look up its time zone on the "Before you go" screen | For map imagery, the area being displayed; for the time zone, the city's coordinates (not your location). Apple's privacy policy applies. |

When you tap links in the app (for example a Wikipedia page, the data sources, a Songkick concert search for a city, or the App Store review page), they open in Safari and are governed by that site's privacy policy.

6. Sharing you start

These features create content on your device, and you decide where it goes. None of it passes through a server of ours.

  • Share images, Year in Review, trip video, PDF travel book, passport stamps: sent to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet.
  • Comparing with a friend (link, QR code or file): contains the list of countries, cities and regions you have visited and the year of your first visit to each. Notes, photos, exact dates and custom places are not included. Anyone who has the link can read this list.
  • Trip invitation: the trip's name, places and dates, optionally the name you type for yourself and the names of the other companions. Notes, photos and ratings are not included. Anyone who has the link can read this information.
  • Backup (JSON/CSV): you choose where to save the file (for example Files or iCloud Drive). Backups do not contain visit photos.
  • Import: files you choose (Google Timeline, exports from other apps, CSV, KML, GPX, GeoJSON) are read on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

7. Support and feedback

"Send feedback" and "Report a bug" open your own mail app with a draft addressed to us. The draft contains the app version, build number, device model and iOS version, but none of your visits, places or photos. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not use this correspondence for any other purpose or share it with third parties, and we delete it within a reasonable time once it is resolved. Write to us if you would like it deleted sooner.

8. Diagnostic logs

To find out why the app may have closed unexpectedly, Wently keeps the crash, hang and performance reports that Apple's MetricKit provides to the app, plus its own short session notes (such as memory use), only on your device. These logs are limited to the most recent few files, contain no visits, places or photos, and are never sent automatically. You can choose to share them yourself from Settings > About > Diagnostic logs.

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us through App Store Connect. This is controlled by Apple and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

9. Children

Wently is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Deleting your data

  • You can delete individual visits, trips, plans and photos in the app.
  • Deleting Wently from your device deletes all of the app's data on that device. Backup files you exported and device backups are not affected; you manage those.
  • The only information we may hold about you is email you sent us; you can ask us by email to delete it.

11. Your rights (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

To exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (access, rectification, erasure, objection, etc.), write to afkaracan@gmail.com. Because we do not keep app data on our servers, such requests in practice cover only email you have sent us. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

12. Security

Your data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption. All of the app's internet connections use HTTPS.

13. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the effective date. Significant changes (such as adding iCloud sync) will also be announced in the app's "What's new" section.
